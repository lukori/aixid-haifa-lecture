# AIxID - i want to be there

A 15-minute talk by Luka Or (Haifa, 3.11.26), converted from `Luka or_AIxID_e.pptx` to one HTML page. Slide 10 (the sketch sheet) was added after the pencil, slides 14-15 (photos) after Level 1, and slide 22 of the pptx was split in two. Slide 17 is new, rebuilt from page 2 of the guidelines PDF. Slides 10-12 of the pptx are 11-13 here, slide 13 of the pptx is 16, and from slide 14 of the pptx to 21, numbers here are 4 higher. The rest runs: 26 Building Our Own Tools for Design (first half of pptx 22), 27 pptx 26, 28 pptx 27, 29 Social Project Based Learning (second half of pptx 22), 30 pptx 23, 31 pptx 24, 32 pptx 25, 33 pptx 28, 34 pptx 29.

Open `index.html` in Chrome (works offline), press **F** for full screen.

- Next: → / ↓ / PageDown / Space / Enter / click. Back: ← / ↑ / PageUp.
- Slide 1 has the HIT and AIxID logos at the bottom, lined up with the left and right edges of the letter grid, on a shared bottom line. The whole composition is centred: 2.9% of the slide width further left than in the pptx, with an extra background copy underneath so the right edge stays covered.
- Slide 2 waits: the first press draws the rings around you, are, here, one by one, overlapping, in about 1.1 seconds (`draw` and `stagger` in the `here` object; before: 550 ms each with no overlap, 2 seconds), and the next moves on.
- Slide 3 draws itself on arrival: "how" is already there, then each line leads to the next word, in about 1 second. A press mid-way completes it, the next moves on. `speed`, `wordIn` and `start` in the `sentence` object set the pace (2 seconds was 6.4 / 60 / 150; 4 seconds was 3.2 / 120 / 300).
- Slide 5: "Specialization is for insects." enters word by word on arrival, 110 ms apart. Its timing is in the `reveal` line. Slide 8: on arrival the strike draws left to right, then "al(most)" writes itself in orange; the first press deletes it backwards and writes "the process", the next moves on. Letters are 38 ms apart (`gap` in the `process` object).
- Slide 11 has a y axis standing apart from the x axis, to its right by a little more than the gap between the orange bar and the end of the x axis (260 units in the `y-axis` path; 0 joined them), labelled "professionalism" at the size of the tool names (Helvetica Neue outlines). It starts with no arrows. The first press draws the short black arrow, which curves into the red bar and runs down inside it (it used to drop between the two bars in 300 ms; that version is in the git history, before commit "Slide 10: keep"), the second the long one, black, a 0.2 second pause, then orange; each stays. The next moves on. Timings are in `presses` in the `trajectory` object.
- Slide 13 (Level 1) is the still illustration with its arrow, no animation. It used to draw the arrow on arrival: 150 ms wait, black 300 ms, 200 ms pause, orange 170 ms, head 70 ms; that version is in the git history, before commit "Slide 12 still".
- Slide 17 shows responsibility over study years, with time running left to right, the lecturer in black and the student in orange, above the seven principles for the student, cut to one line each. Everything on it is live text and lines in the page, with no press.
- Slide 18 (slide 14 in the pptx) has "the student" at the top centre throughout and starts with "It's all about you". Presses show "It's not magic" and "S#@IT in S#@IT out" with no animation, the next draws the arrow right to left, and the next moves on. Its text is drawn as shapes (Helvetica Neue outlines), so it looks the same on any computer.
- Slide 24 (Level 2, slide 20 in the pptx) starts with only the box and crank. On arrival the crank turns once, then the spring pops out with the plate on top, overshoots and settles. `turn`, `bounce` and `damping` in the `jack` object set the feel.
- Slide 25 (21 in the pptx): on arrival the arrow draws left to right, then the dot appears with "vibe coding" under it.
- Slide 30 loops silently on its own. Slide 32 waits: the first press plays the film with sound, the next moves on. Moving the mouse on slide 32 shows a timeline at the bottom: click or drag it to jump, with no effect on the slide count; it hides after 1.5 seconds without movement, so a clicker never shows it.
- Links on slides 27, 28, 30 and 34 open in a new tab and need internet. On 27 and 28 a click on an image opens its link too, so on 27, where the image fills the slide, move on with the keyboard or clicker. Closing the tab returns to the same slide; full screen may need F again.
- Slide 28 (27 in the pptx) has three images with links: AI Photo Studio, Image to Hand Study Sketch and sphericon. The row runs between the left and right margins of the slide, and each caption sits under the left edge of its image.
- An image that fails to load tries again up to 5 times, because GitHub Pages sometimes answers 503.
- `#N` in the address opens slide N.

`assets/` holds the deck's original images and videos. The film on slide 32 comes from the copy embedded in the pptx, not a YouTube stream; it was re-encoded (keyframe every 2 seconds, no B-frames) after one report of audio playing over a frozen first frame, which did not reproduce in testing.
